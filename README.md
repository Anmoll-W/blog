# Building in Public

Writing about systems, silent bugs, and shipping products with AI tools.

I am Anmoll, a product manager who builds with Claude Code, Lovable, Supabase, and Vercel. These posts come from real working sessions in my Obsidian vault. Every post is distilled from something that actually happened: a bug that took too long to find, a decision that needed to be made, a system that needed to be rebuilt.
